## Source Summary
1. **Content:** seven AAS submodel templates that cover the data points of the EU Battery Regulation, aligned with DIN DKE SPEC 99100.
2. **Ecosystem:** AAS presented as the data format for the Catena-X and Factory-X data spaces, from which the Digital Product Passport is assembled.
3. **Caveat:** industry association documents; no public figures on how many passports are live.
