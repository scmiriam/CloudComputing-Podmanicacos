## Source Summary
1. **Deprecated:** Launcher, Exchange app installs, Nucleus Workstation (replaced by Hub Workstation Cache; Enterprise Nucleus Server is still available through NGC).
2. **New path:** Omniverse Kit SDK, apps, samples and templates on GitHub and the NGC Catalog.
3. **Positioning:** Kit for robotics, autonomous vehicles and industrial digital twins.
