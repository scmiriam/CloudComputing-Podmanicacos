## Source Summary
1. **Announcement:** BMW's virtual factory presented in an NVIDIA GTC keynote demo.
2. **Technology:** Omniverse bridges data from Siemens Process Simulate, Autodesk Revit and Bentley Systems MicroStation; BMW built a custom app, Factory Explorer, on Omniverse USD Composer.
3. **Caveat:** NVIDIA's account of the announcement, not BMW's own press release or an independent evaluation.
