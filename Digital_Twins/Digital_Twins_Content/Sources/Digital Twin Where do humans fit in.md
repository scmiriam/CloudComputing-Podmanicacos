# [Source] - Digital Twin: Where do humans fit in? (Agrawal et al., 2023)

- **Author/Entity:** Ashwin Agrawal, Robert Thiel, Pooja Jain, Vishal Singh & Martin Fischer
- **Year:** 2023 (*Automation in Construction*, Vol. 148, Article 104749, Elsevier; preprint on arXiv)
- **Focus:** Which roles a digital twin can play in a work system and how far each role can be automated
- **Key Quote:**
  - *"A particular DT can play any number of roles at varying levels."*

## Source Summary
A peer-reviewed paper that proposes the two-dimensional **Levels of Digital Twin (LoDT)** framework:

1. **Problem:** Digital twins are implemented piecemeal; some functions are automated while others remain human. Unclear role allocation causes significant costs, misallocated resources, unrealistic expectations and strategic misalignment.
2. **Research question:** When humans work with digital twins, which roles can a twin play, and to what extent can each role be automated?
3. **Dimension 1, four roles:** Observer, Analyst, Decision Maker and Action Executor.
4. **Dimension 2, five automation levels:** No automation, routine support, routine autonomy, non-routine support and non-routine autonomy.
5. **Use:** A case study shows how the framework helps practitioners plan deployments, communicate goals and deliverables, and set a strategic vision.
