## Source Summary
1. **Type:** Cross-sector review (over 2,200 citations on Semantic Scholar, November 2026).
2. **Use in our presentation:** Useful for the challenges block and to justify our choice of sectors (manufacturing, healthcare, smart cities).
3. **Challenge Taxonomy (Section V of the paper):** Identifies seven cross-cutting challenges shared across IIoT/AI and Digital Twins:
   - **IT Infrastructure:** High-performance compute (GPUs/cloud) and uninterrupted low-latency networking required to run real-time analytics and update models continuously.
   - **Useful Data:** Filtering noise, incompleteness, and unstructured streams so big data fed into the twin is high-quality and actionable.
   - **Privacy and Security:** Protecting high-value operational and personal data streams from cyberattacks and complying with data regulations.
   - **Trust:** Overcoming organizational and user skepticism through transparent validation and privacy-by-design architectures.
   - **Expectations:** Managing commercial hype versus realistic ROI and maturity limits.
   - **Standardised Modelling:** Addressing the lack of universal protocols, data formats, and architectural standards from sensor to cloud.
   - **Domain Modelling:** Transferring multi-disciplinary domain knowledge accurately into digital models across different sectors.
