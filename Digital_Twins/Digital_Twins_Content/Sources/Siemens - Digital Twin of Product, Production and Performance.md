# [Source] - The Digital Twin: Product, Production and Performance | Siemens

- **Author/Entity:** Siemens AG (corporate digital twin page) and CIMdata's commentary *Siemens PLM Software: The Actionable, Holistic Digital Twin*
- **Year:** CIMdata commentary published October 2017; Siemens web page consulted in 2026
- **Focus:** Functional classification of twins along the industrial value chain
- **Key Quote:**
  - *"constantly fed with operational data from products or the production plant"* (Siemens, on the performance twin)

## Source Summary
Siemens' industrial framing of the digital twin as three connected, function-specific twins, complemented by an independent analyst's commentary:

1. **Product twin:** Created from the definition and design stage onwards to test and optimize mechanics, electronics, software and system performance before production.
2. **Production twin:** Represents machines, plant controllers and entire production lines virtually, enabling automation code generation and virtual commissioning before the real plant starts.
3. **Performance twin:** Continuously fed with operational data from products and plants to support predictive maintenance, energy optimization and data-driven business models.
4. **Closed loop:** Knowledge from operations is fed back through the value chain to the product and production twins for continuous optimization.
5. **CIMdata's analysis (2017):** Describes the combination of functional twins as an "actionable, holistic" digital twin that helps create a digital thread across the product and process lifecycle, and defines a digital twin as a dynamic virtual representation of the product, the production process or the product's utilization, accurate enough to predict real performance.
6. **Caveat:** A vendor taxonomy aligned with Siemens' software portfolio; useful as a value-chain lens, but not a standard.
