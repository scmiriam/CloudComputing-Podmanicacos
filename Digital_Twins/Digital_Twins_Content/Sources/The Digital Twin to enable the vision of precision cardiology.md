# [Source] - The Digital Twin to enable the vision of precision cardiology

- **Author/Entity:** Jorge Corral-Acero, Francesca Margara, Maciej Marciniak, et al. (39 authors; last author Pablo Lamata) / Academic and industry consortium (King's College London, University of Oxford and others)
- **Year:** 2020
- **Published in:** European Heart Journal, 41(48), pp. 4556–4564
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehaa159
- **Focus:** Position Paper / Healthcare
- **Key Points (from the abstract):**
  - Precision medicine needs not only patient data but computers and algorithms able to learn, reason and build a digital twin of each patient.
  - Reviews the first steps of digital twins in cardiovascular medicine.
  - Discusses challenges and opportunities ahead.
  - Stresses the synergy between mechanistic (physics-based) and statistical (data-driven) models.
