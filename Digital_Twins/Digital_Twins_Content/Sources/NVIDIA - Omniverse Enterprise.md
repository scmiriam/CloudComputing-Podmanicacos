# [Source] - NVIDIA Omniverse Enterprise | NVIDIA

- **Author/Entity:** NVIDIA
- **Date:** consulted 11 Nov 2026
- **URL:** https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/omniverse/ (FAQ section)
- **Old URL:** https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/omniverse/enterprise/ (no longer exists; redirects to the general Omniverse page, checked 13 Nov 2026)
- **Also:** https://forums.developer.nvidia.com/t/updated-pricing-plans-for-omniverse-enterprise/282857
- **Focus:** Licensing and price
- **Key Quotes:**
  - *"The NVIDIA Omniverse Enterprise software subscription is $4,500 per GPU per year."*

## Source Summary
1. **Licensing:** changed from "per named user" to **per GPU**. Multi-year and perpetual options exist.
2. **Resellers (2026):** about €5,800 per GPU/year in Europe, bundled with NVIDIA AI Enterprise; academic licenses around $1,200 per GPU/year.
3. **Caveat:** a secondary article says production use is now free and only support is paid. This could not be confirmed with an NVIDIA source, so the official list price is used.

## Update (checked 13 Nov 2026)
The Omniverse Enterprise page no longer exists. The official Omniverse FAQ now states that, as of May 2026, Omniverse is *"freely available for both development and production use"*, with no NVIDIA AI Enterprise subscription required, and software built with it can be redistributed freely (subject to the NVIDIA EULA). Without a subscription, support is community-only (Developer Forum and Discord); enterprise support requires an NVIDIA AI Enterprise subscription. This confirms the caveat in point 3: the per-GPU prices above are historical and no longer apply to using Omniverse itself.
