# [Source] - Digital Twin in Industry State-of-the-Art

- **Author/Entity:** Fei Tao, He Zhang, Ang Liu, A. Y. C. Nee / Beihang University / UNSW / National University of Singapore
- **Year:** 2019
- **Published in:** IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, 15(4), pp. 2405–2415
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/TII.2018.2873186
- **Focus:** Literature Review / Industry
- **Key Points (from the abstract):**
  - Reviews the state of the art of digital twin research: key components, current development and major industrial applications.
  - Main application areas: product design, production, and prognostics and health management (PHM).
  - Claims to be the first review focused specifically on digital twin applications in industry.
  - Closes with current challenges and future research directions.
