# [Source] - The Living Heart Project A robust and integrative simulator for human heart function

- **Author/Entity:** Brian Baillargeon, Nuno Rebelo, David D. Fox, Robert L. Taylor, Ellen Kuhl / Dassault Systèmes SIMULIA / University of California, Berkeley / Stanford University
- **Year:** 2014
- **Published in:** European Journal of Mechanics - A/Solids, vol. 48, pp. 38–47
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euromechsol.2014.04.001
- **Focus:** Technical / Healthcare
- **Key Points (from the abstract):**
  - Proof-of-concept simulator of a four-chamber human heart built from computed tomography and magnetic resonance images.
  - Couples electrical excitation and mechanical contraction in a single finite element environment.
  - Shows electrical potential and mechanical deformation across the cardiac cycle; the pressure-volume relationship agrees well with clinical observations.
  - Intended to guide device design and treatment planning for stenosis, regurgitation or prolapse of the four heart valves.

## Source Summary
1. **Contribution:** A realistic, validated whole-heart model, the basis of Dassault Systèmes' Living Heart Project.
2. **Digital twin angle:** A generic heart simulation that is the starting point for patient-specific twins.
